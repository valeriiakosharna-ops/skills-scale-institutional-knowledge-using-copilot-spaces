# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers oversee release planning, coordination, and production deployment. They ensure smooth transitions to production, maintain release schedules, and serve as the central point of escalation for release-related blockers and issues.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate release schedules and deployment windows
- Ensure pre-release requirements are met (testing, documentation, security scans)
- Prepare and maintain release notes and deployment runbooks
- Execute or oversee deployment to staging and production environments
- Conduct post-deployment verifications and monitoring
- Create and maintain rollback and mitigation plans
- Track release blockers and escalate as needed
- Communicate release status to stakeholders and support teams

### Goals
- Minimize deployment risk and production incidents
- Achieve consistent, predictable release cycles
- Maintain clear visibility into release readiness and status
- Enable rapid rollback when necessary

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with developers and QA
- Pre-release checklists and status updates
- Deployment window announcements and incident communications
- Post-release retrospectives and incident reviews

### Interactions
- **With Developers:** Ensures code is production-ready, coordinates deployment timing, and addresses deployment-related issues
- **With QA/Testing:** Validates quality gates have been met and coordinates acceptance testing
- **With Product Manager:** Communicates release timing, customer-facing features, and any delays or risks
- **With Project Manager:** Manages release timeline conflicts and escalates blockers

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create intuitive, accessible, and visually compelling user experiences. They ensure that all digital touchpoints meet usability standards and align with the product vision.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing to inform design decisions
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups
- Define and maintain design systems and style guides
- Review designs for accessibility and usability compliance
- Provide design feedback and guidance during development
- Iterate on designs based on user feedback and metrics
- Collaborate on design documentation and design-to-development handoffs

### Goals
- Deliver user-centric, accessible interfaces
- Reduce user friction and improve adoption rates
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Enable developers to implement features accurately

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Design specifications and component libraries
- Usability testing reports and recommendations
- Collaboration with developers on implementation details

### Interactions
- **With Product Manager:** Aligns on user needs, priorities, and success metrics for designs
- **With Developers:** Ensures designs are feasible, provides detailed specifications, and supports implementation
- **With QA/Testing:** Validates that implemented features match design intent and usability standards
- **With Stakeholders:** Presents design rationale and user research findings

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts provide data-driven insights to inform planning, prioritization, and decision-making. They monitor key metrics post-launch and help measure the impact of product initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs) and success metrics
- Create dashboards and reports for visibility into product health
- Analyze user behavior and product usage data
- Provide insights and recommendations to inform roadmap priorities
- Conduct post-launch analysis to measure feature impact
- Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities for improvement
- Support A/B testing and experimentation frameworks

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making across the organization
- Provide clear visibility into product performance and user behavior
- Identify growth opportunities and risks early
- Support measurement of project outcomes and ROI

### Typical Communication
- Weekly/monthly dashboards and metric reports
- Data-driven recommendations and insights
- Analysis of feature performance and user adoption
- Ad-hoc data queries and deep-dive analyses

### Interactions
- **With Product Manager:** Advises on metrics definition, reports on feature performance, and supports prioritization decisions
- **With Project Manager:** Provides data on project health and impact measurement for stakeholder reporting
- **With Developers:** Works on instrumentation and data pipeline needs
- **With the whole team:** Shares dashboards and interprets data for collective understanding

---

## Customer Support Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Support Liaisons act as the bridge between the support organization and the delivery team. They represent customer feedback in planning and ensure that user-facing issues are triaged, communicated, and resolved effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and prioritize customer feedback and support tickets
- Escalate critical issues and known bugs to the delivery team
- Communicate customer impact and urgency for issues
- Coordinate support communications for incidents and releases
- Help document known issues and workarounds
- Provide customer context for feature prioritization
- Assist in post-incident reviews and customer communications

### Goals
- Reduce customer impact from bugs and issues
- Ensure customer feedback informs product decisions
- Maintain transparency and trust with customers during incidents
- Enable faster resolution of customer-facing problems

### Typical Communication
- Weekly support ticket summaries and issue escalations
- Customer feedback and feature request compilations
- Incident communications and status updates
- Post-release support readiness coordination

### Interactions
- **With Product Manager:** Provides customer feedback loops and context for prioritization decisions
- **With Project Manager:** Coordinates incident communication and escalates customer-impacting blockers
- **With Developers:** Provides customer context, helps reproduce issues, and coordinates urgent fixes
- **With QA/Testing:** Shares customer-reported scenarios and edge cases for testing
- **With Release Manager:** Prepares support team for releases and coordinates customer communications

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When planning projects, ensure the appropriate personas are assigned based on project scope and complexity.
- Cross-functional collaboration between these personas ensures comprehensive project planning, execution, and delivery.
